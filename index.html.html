01 · Who I Am

From CAD Lines
to Cost Intelligence

My path into cost consulting began on the drawing board — learning how buildings are designed before understanding how they're priced. That dual literacy, reading structure and reading budgets, became my core professional asset. I hold a B.Tech in Civil Engineering (81%, First Division) from Goel Institute of Technology & Management, Lucknow — Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Technical University.

At Grant Thornton Bharat LLP, I serve as an independent Cost Consultant embedded on behalf of lender institutions. My mandate: verify project costs, flag deviations, and ensure capital disbursed matches construction delivered. For clients like Godrej Properties and M3M India, approximations are not acceptable — the numbers must be defensible.

My differentiator is methodology. I integrate Revit BIM directly into the QS workflow — extracting quantities from model geometry rather than scaling 2D drawings manually. Across 1M+ sq ft of high-rise built-up area, that approach has consistently delivered ±2% cost accuracy and 25% faster turnaround compared to traditional takeoff methods.

Why BIM Improves Quantity Surveying Efficiency

Traditional quantity takeoff often depends entirely on manual scaling from 2D CAD or PDF drawings, which can become time-consuming, difficult to update, and prone to discrepancies when designs evolve. In my workflow, I combine CAD-based measurement methods with Revit-driven quantity extraction to improve accuracy, traceability, and efficiency on large-scale projects. Where Revit models are available, quantities are extracted directly from model geometry and linked element data, allowing faster updates and better coordination during design revisions. For drawing-based projects, I use structured CAD workflows and plugins to maintain consistency and detailed verification across disciplines. This hybrid approach helps deliver audit-ready BOQs, accurate cost reports, and dependable quantity intelligence for high-rise and lender-monitored projects where precision directly impacts cost control and decision-making.
